RealitySucksRP Miami Edition artwork
====================================

The active NUI uses these locally bundled WebP files:

  rs-girl-left.webp        left-side character artwork
  rs-guy-right.webp        right-side character artwork
  rs-los-santos-seal.webp  centre seal
  rs-main-chair.webp        cinematic scene background

They are referenced by ../index.html and ../main.css. Keep the filenames stable
unless both references are updated. The NUI does not download artwork at runtime.
